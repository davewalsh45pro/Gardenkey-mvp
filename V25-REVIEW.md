# GardenKey V25 review

V25 separates pots from borders without changing the underlying principle: a specific My Plant record can be linked into either type of growing space. Existing V23 pot data remains under the original local-storage keys and will continue to appear in My Pots. New borders use separate keys, preventing the two lists from becoming mixed.

The Aeonium tester draft is now represented by a complete approved plant guide. The personal provenance note was retained as a tester observation, while the care fields were written as general guidance suitable for the shared library.
